Exodus

In a world touched by darkness, light was still given.

There were moments when no path could be seen. Every turn felt unknown, every step indescribable, every silence heavy with the question of what would come next. Yet even in the absence of direction, there remained a quiet promise: light does not always arrive all at once. Sometimes it appears as a thread. Sometimes as a color. Sometimes as the smallest sign that the journey is not over.

Exodus was created through that belief.

Hand-tufted with intention, each strand was pushed into place as a meditation on movement, survival, and becoming. The process required patience — building the form slowly, shaping the surface, carving the details, and allowing the work to reveal itself layer by layer. What began as yarn became texture. What began as texture became a passage. What began as a passage became a testimony of hope, grace, and beauty.

This piece speaks to the moment between leaving and arriving. The space where the old world has fallen behind, but the new one has not yet fully appeared. It honors those who keep walking without certainty, those who carry faith through the unseen, and those who trust that even when the road is hidden, a path will eventually show.

Exodus is a reminder that darkness is not the end of the story.

Light was given.
Grace remained.
Beauty survived.

And the way forward was formed, one step — one thread — at a time.

Chronicles

There are stories that are not written in one day.

Chronicles speaks to the sacred record of becoming — the chapters we survive, the weight we carry, the strength we build when life asks us to keep standing. Like the books of Chronicles in scripture, this piece reflects legacy, remembrance, lineage, and the endurance required to continue forward when the story is still being written.

For Black Lobby Collective, Chronicles is personal. It reflects the making of a brand built by hand, by faith, by patience, and by the quiet refusal to disappear. Every thread carries a record. Every carved line becomes evidence. Every texture holds the memory of process — the pushing, shaping, trimming, refining, and returning again until the piece reveals its full presence.

This work is about strength, but not strength without feeling.
It is about endurance with grace.
It is about embracing the chapters that shaped us, even the ones we did not understand while living through them.

Chronicles is a reminder that nothing endured is wasted.

The story remains.
The record stands.

And what was built through pressure becomes beautiful.

Psalms

Some pieces are not only made to be seen.
They are made to be remembered.

Psalms draws from the sacred language of praise, grief, courage, worship, and glory. Like the biblical Psalms, this piece holds both the cry and the song — the moments of uncertainty, the moments of gratitude, and the moment when remembrance becomes strength.

Through the hand-tufting process, every thread was placed like a verse. Every color became a reflection. Every carved detail became rhythm. The work slowly transformed from material into testimony, carrying the spirit of a song that does not need sound to be heard.

For Black Lobby Collective, Psalms honors the emotional architecture of the brand — art that remembers where it came from, praises what carried it, and gives glory to the beauty that survives. It is a piece for the collector who understands that luxury is not only about possession. It is about presence, meaning, and the story a work brings into a room.

Psalms reflects remembrance.
It reflects story.
It reflects praise, glory, and courage.

It is a reminder that even after silence, the spirit can still sing.
Even after darkness, beauty can still rise.
Even after the unknown, there is still something worthy of praise.

The Book of Ruth

Queen Vashti

There are paths we walk without knowing what they will restore.

Book of Ruth is inspired by one of scripture's most tender stories of loyalty, devotion, loss, faith, and divine alignment. Ruth's journey is not loud. It is steady. It is graceful. It is a story of choosing to keep going when life has changed, of entering unfamiliar fields, and of trusting that provision can still meet us along the way.

This piece reflects that quiet courage.

In the tufting process, every thread was placed with patience, as if following a path not fully revealed at once. The work unfolded slowly — layer by layer, cut by cut, detail by detail. What began as yarn became a field of texture. What began as process became a story of restoration.

For Black Lobby Collective, Book of Ruth speaks to the beauty of remaining faithful to the work. To building something by hand when the outcome is not yet visible. To trusting that grace is still moving, even when the road feels uncertain.

This piece is about devotion.
It is about beauty after loss.
It is about the courage to step into a new chapter and believe that something meaningful can still bloom there.

Book of Ruth is not only a rug.

It is a symbol of grace meeting endurance.

A reminder that what is meant to find you can still arrive — softly, beautifully, and in its appointed time.
