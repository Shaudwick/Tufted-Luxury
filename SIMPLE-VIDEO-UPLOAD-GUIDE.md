# Simple Guide: How to Upload Your Video File

## Step 1: Find Out Who Hosts Your Website

Your website is at **blacklobby.co**. To upload files, you need to know who hosts it.

### How to Find Out:

1. **Check your email** - Look for emails from:
   - GoDaddy
   - Bluehost
   - HostGator
   - Namecheap
   - Squarespace
   - Wix
   - Or any other hosting company

2. **Check your domain registrar** - Where did you buy "blacklobby.co"?
   - That company might also host your website

3. **Ask someone** - If someone else set up your website, ask them!

---

## Step 2: The Easiest Way to Upload (Web-Based File Manager)

Most hosting companies have a **web-based file manager** - this is the easiest way!

### What You Need:
- Your hosting account login (username/password)
- Access to your hosting control panel

### Steps:

1. **Log into your hosting account**
   - Go to your hosting company's website
   - Click "Login" or "My Account"
   - Enter your username and password

2. **Find "File Manager" or "Files"**
   - Look for buttons like:
     - "File Manager"
     - "Files"
     - "Website Files"
     - "cPanel" (then look for "File Manager")

3. **Navigate to your website folder**
   - Look for a folder called:
     - `public_html`
     - `www`
     - `htdocs`
     - Or your domain name folder
   - Inside that, find `Website-Images` folder

4. **Upload the video**
   - Click "Upload" button
   - Select the file: `BCLAS.mp4` (from your computer)
   - Wait for upload to complete (261MB will take a few minutes)
   - Make sure it's in the `Website-Images` folder

---

## Step 3: If You Can't Find File Manager

### Option A: Contact Your Hosting Provider

**Call or email your hosting company** and say:
> "Hi, I need to upload a video file (261MB) to my website. Can you help me access my file manager or provide FTP credentials?"

They will help you!

### Option B: Use a Simple FTP Program

If your hosting company gives you FTP credentials, you can use:

1. **Download FileZilla** (free): https://filezilla-project.org/download.php?type=client

2. **Get FTP info from your hosting company:**
   - FTP Host/Server: (they'll tell you)
   - Username: (they'll tell you)
   - Password: (they'll tell you)
   - Port: Usually 21

3. **Connect in FileZilla:**
   - Enter the info they gave you
   - Click "Quickconnect"
   - Navigate to `Website-Images` folder
   - Drag and drop `BCLAS.mp4` from your computer

---

## Step 4: Verify It Worked

After uploading, test if the video is accessible:

1. Open your web browser
2. Go to: `https://www.blacklobby.co/Website-Images/BCLAS.mp4`
3. If the video plays, you're done! ✅
