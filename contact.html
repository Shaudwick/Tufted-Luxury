Commission a Custom Rug

Work directly with our artisans to create a one-of-a-kind tufted rug tailored to your space. We guide you through material selection, sizing, design consultation, and installation. Lead time: 6-12 weeks. Starting at $1,200.

Workshops for Colleges & Universities

We offer hands-on workshops on traditional tufting techniques, contemporary design, and the intersection of craft and culture. Our interactive workshops provide students with practical experience in artisanal craftsmanship, material selection, design process, and cultural heritage. Learning outcomes include understanding traditional techniques, contemporary applications, and the business of craft.

Formats: 2-4 hour workshops, multi-day residencies

AV Needs: Projector, screen (for demonstrations)

Travel Policy: We travel nationwide. Travel and accommodation included in fee structure.

Fees: Starting at $2,500 (varies by format, duration, and location)

Venue Partnerships

Transform your space with rotating art installations of our handcrafted tufted rugs. We provide curated pieces, installation, placards with QR codes linking to story pages, and regular rotation to keep your space fresh and engaging.

Wedding Receptions

Transform your wedding reception into an unforgettable experience with our handcrafted tufted rugs as stunning centerpieces, ceremony backdrops, or elegant floor installations. Each piece is curated to complement your wedding theme and create a luxurious, artisanal atmosphere that your guests will remember.
