What We Offer

Commission a Custom Rug

Work directly with our artisans to create a one-of-a-kind tufted rug tailored to your space. We guide you through material selection, sizing, design consultation, and installation. Lead time: 6-12 weeks. Starting at $1,200.

Workshops for Colleges & Universities

Hands-on workshops on traditional tufting techniques, contemporary design, and the intersection of craft and culture. Formats: 2-4 hour workshops, multi-day residencies. AV Needs: Projector, screen. Travel: Nationwide, included in fee. Fees: Starting at $2,500.

Wedding Receptions

Transform your wedding with handcrafted tufted rugs as centerpieces, ceremony backdrops, or floor installations. What We Offer: Custom installations, ceremony backdrops, reception centerpieces, photo backdrops. Services Include: Design consultation, professional installation and removal. Custom pricing based on your needs.

Artwork Installation Rotation Package

Transform your space with rotating art installations of our handcrafted tufted rugs. We provide curated pieces, professional installation, placards with QR codes linking to story pages, and regular rotation to keep your space fresh and engaging.
