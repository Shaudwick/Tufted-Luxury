Sun Card

Some mornings arrive slowly. The light comes first — not as a flood, but as a thread across the wall.

Sun Card holds that moment. The sun is tufted in deep gold, its rays reaching outward with warmth and certainty. Beside it, a figure draped in red — presence, protection, and the quiet courage of showing up fully.

Every loop was placed by hand. The cream ground softens the composition. Green leaves and red accents mark the space between radiance and embodiment — the place where light meets life.

Framed in black with gold corner details, this piece is made for altars, entryways, studios, and rooms that need a daily reminder: warmth is still given. Beauty still rises. The day can begin again.

Sun Card is a small collection with a large presence.

Light was given.
Devotion remained.
And the frame holds what matters.
