Sun Card

Some mornings arrive slowly. The light comes first — not as a flood, but as a thread across the wall.

Sun Card holds that moment. The sun is tufted in deep gold, its rays reaching outward with warmth and certainty. Beside it, a figure draped in red — presence, protection, and the quiet courage of showing up fully.

Every loop was placed by hand. The cream ground softens the composition. Green leaves and red accents mark the space between radiance and embodiment — the place where light meets life.

Sun Card is warmth you can hang on the wall.

Light was given.
Devotion remained.
And the frame holds what matters.

The Magician

Before the work becomes visible, there is a moment of gathering — tools aligned, attention focused, intention named.

The Magician holds that threshold. A card about manifestation: what you reach for, what you shape, and what you are willing to make real with your hands.

Tufted thread by thread, the piece becomes a reminder that craft is a form of prayer — patient, deliberate, and alive with possibility.

The Magician does not rush the reveal.

Intention is set.
Skill is honored.
And the work begins.
